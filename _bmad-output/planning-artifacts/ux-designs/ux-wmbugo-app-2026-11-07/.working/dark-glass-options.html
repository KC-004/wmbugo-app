ALight-mode reference (PRD glass)

The PRD's existing glass.surface: white at 18% with blur and a light border, over the gradient.brand hero band. Shown in light theme so you can see what each dark option is translating.

Trade-off: looks bright and on-brand, but the white text depends on where the glass sits on the gradient.

Contrast at risk. White text on the glass is 5.59:1 over #164E63, 3.84:1 over #0F766E (fails 4.5:1 for body text) and 1.68:1 over #2DD4BF. The hero must sit on the deep-cyan end of the gradient, or the glass needs more tint, to meet PRD §6.6.

BSmoked dark glass

The glass becomes a translucent near-black (#0F172A at 62%) with a faint light border, so it reads as dark tinted glass over dim brand-gradient glows. Text contrast is the highest of the glass options, and the screen stays quiet at night.

Trade-off: most legible and calm, but the brand colour shows only in the glows behind the glass, so it can look like generic dark mode. Blur still costs GPU and battery, the same as C.

Contrast OK. Worst case (over the brightest glow): primary 12.11:1, secondary 8.53:1, warning 8.79:1.

CTeal-tinted glass

The glass is translucent deep cyan (#164E63 at 62%) with a faint teal border, so the hero and tab bar stay brand-coloured in dark mode. It is the closest dark match to the light-mode glass over the gradient.

Trade-off: strongest brand feel, but lowest contrast of the dark options, and the glass colour shifts with whatever glows behind it. Same blur GPU and battery cost as B.

Watch secondary text. Worst case (over the #2DD4BF glow): primary 7.53:1, secondary 5.31:1, warning 5.46:1. This passes, but with little margin. If the glow goes above about 45% opacity or the tint below about 55%, secondary text drops toward 4.5:1, so glow strength must be capped as a token.

DSolid in dark (fallback as default)

No blur in dark mode: the hero and tab bar use an opaque teal-tinted surface (#1A3C4F, a 50/50 mix of #164E63 and #1E293B), with a faint teal border and a soft gradient wash at the top of the screen. It is the same surface as glass.fallback, so dark mode and battery-saver look the same.

Trade-off: no GPU or battery cost, contrast that never changes, and one fewer state for QA (M4 sessions), but it gives up the floating, layered look the brand uses in light mode, so the two themes feel less alike.

Contrast OK. Primary 11.13:1, secondary 7.84:1, warning 8.08:1. These are fixed values because nothing shows through the surface.

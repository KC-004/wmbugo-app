# Input reconciliation — PRD v1.3

**Method.** Every DEC, SCR, FIN, ONB, ACC, TXN, CAT, BDG, REC, GOAL, HOM, INS, IMP, OCR, SMS, SET, BKP, SEC, CAP and LRN ID, FLOW, NFR, GATE, test suite, P0 criterion, epic, entity and glossary term in v1.2 was looked up in v1.3. Every `outcome` entry and every decision in the session log was checked against the PRD and addendum. Banter was ignored.

**Headline.** Nothing in v1.2 was silently deleted by ID: all v1.2 IDs (DEC-01..25, SCR-01..29, FLOW-01..12, NFR-01..15, GATE-01..11, all 16 test suites, all P0 lines, WMB-01..16, all §11 entities) still exist, either unchanged or amended with a change-log entry. The gaps are (a) v1.2 text left stale next to v1.3 changes, so the PRD now contradicts itself, (b) a few behaviours weakened during rewriting with no change-log note, (c) a few session decisions recorded loosely or not at all, and (d) tone and copy rules that sit only in the addendum, which says it is non-binding.

## Intent

Implements the epic's Story 2.6 acceptance criteria (see epics.md).

## Implementation Notes

- Migration `0005_steep_hex` (generated by drizzle-kit) adds `account.statement_set_at`. The three card columns (`credit_limit_minor`, `statement_due_minor`, `statement_due_date`) already existed from the first schema, so only the "when was the statement entered" column was needed, to decide what "paid" means.
- Core `ledger/cardDetails.ts`: `UpdateCardDetails` (card only; zero amount or limit refused; clearing a field removes it; due date validated; audit + `AccountsChanged`), `CARD_DETAILS_SQL` (details plus money moved onto the card by transfer since the statement was entered), `statementStatus` (derived on read: none, due, overdue, paid; never stored).
- The limit and statement amount never enter any total: a unit test sets them and shows In accounts, Card dues, the groups and net worth are unchanged. Card dues stays the sum of ledger outstanding.
- Mobile account detail for a card: balance reads `You owe ₹12,450` with the caption `From your entries in wmbugo`; `Statement due ₹8,200 · by 15 Nov`, `Statement paid · ₹8,200` (stays until edited or cleared) or `Statement due ₹8,200 · was due 15 Nov` with a warning icon in primary text; `Limit ₹1,00,000` as its own line. `Card details` opens the panel (prefilled, each field optional, says `You enter these from your statement. wmbugo isn't connected to your bank.`). A zero limit shows `Error: enter a limit or leave it empty`; a zero statement shows the matching error.
- A card above zero already reads `In credit` in `Money you owe` (Story 2.2) and adds nothing to Card dues or Money you have.

## Deviation

- `Pay card bill` is in the AC's action list but is Story 2.7, which is next; `Edit` is the `Card details` button until Story 2.9's edit screen.
- A paid statement counts only transfers that arrive after the statement was entered, by entry time.

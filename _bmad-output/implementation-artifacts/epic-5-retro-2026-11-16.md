## Findings

Sources are the spec Review Triage Logs under `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/spec-5-*.md` and the diff.

### Aggregate views
1. **Chokepoint design worked for hide amounts (accept).** Masking goes through `Amount`, `moneyText`, `LimitBar` and `labels.ts`, with `moneyLeaks.testkit.ts` proving no money is left. Source: story 5.3 commit `53250db`. Reviewers still found foreign-amount leaks in the Activity row label and review card; those were fixed. Lesson: any new place that formats money outside the chokepoints will leak. The test kit is the guard.
2. **Growth is spread, not concentrated.** The largest non-test files are `HomeEditScreen.tsx` (325 lines), `HomeCards.tsx` (321, three commits), `HeroCard.tsx` (189) and `BarChart.tsx` (174). No god class. `HomeCards.tsx` holds four-plus cards and is the one to watch (defer).
3. **`copy.ts` grew in all six stories (+70 lines, 6 commits).** Single-file conflict hotspot, and the copy catalogue test forced rewording twice (capital-first, letters-only keys). Defer: split by feature when it next hurts.
4. **No new dependencies in Epic 5** (no `package.json` change in the range). This is the direct result of replacing Victory Native XL with plain views.
5. **No raw number formatting in non-test code:** a scan of added lines for `toLocaleString`, `toFixed(` and `Intl.NumberFormat` found nothing.

### Spec-to-implementation reconciliation
- **Charts library deviation (needs a human decision):** story 5.5 named Victory Native XL; `<BarChart>` in `apps/mobile/src/shared/ui/BarChart.tsx` uses plain views because Skia and Reanimated cannot be built or verified on this PC. Behaviour matches the spec (table view, labels). Propose recording as an accepted deviation or adding a follow-up story.
- **No migrations:** 5.3 and 5.6 stored state as JSON settings keys (`ui.hide_amounts`, `ui.home_layout`) where the story text mentioned migrations. Accept; record in the stories.
- **Table-view persistence:** "kept until Home is left" and "Home unchanged on Back" conflict; implemented as kept while Home is mounted. Accept and reconcile the spec wording.

### Review lenses (the reviewers' findings, all fixed with tests)
- Failed Edit Home save closed the screen and lost edits (5.6); the unmount save could fire with a half-edited layout (5.6); drag heights were kept by index (5.6).
- Fail-open on a settings load error for hide amounts (5.3); failed-save revert race (5.3).
- Stale `Set a limit` request (5.2); zero-limit showed a full bar (5.2).
- A failed refresh replaced good cards (5.4).
- Home settings read looped the app shell (found by the App tests); fixed with `retryOnMount: false`.
- Pattern: most real defects were failure and race paths in state handling, not happy paths. Every one came from an independent reviewer, none from the author's tests first.

### Verification gaps
- Drag-and-drop glue in `HomeEditScreen.tsx` is untested beyond the arithmetic; Move up, Move down and TalkBack actions are the tested path (deferred in 5.6 triage).
- Nothing in Epic 5 has run on a device. TalkBack, large-font and visual checks of the hero bar, charts and Edit Home are all unverified.
- Full-suite flakiness was masked, not removed: `testTimeout: 20000` in `apps/mobile/jest.config.js`. Defer: find the slow screen tests.
